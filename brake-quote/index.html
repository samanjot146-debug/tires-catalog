Rotor Replacement

  • Eliminates vibration and pedal pulsing

Not sure if your brakes need service? Our comprehensive brake inspection covers pads, rotors, calipers, brake lines, hardware, and fluid condition. We give you an honest, no-pressure assessment — and only recommend what your vehicle actually needs.

Brake Fluid Flush

Brake fluid absorbs moisture over time, which lowers its boiling point and reduces braking performance — especially critical during heavy braking on Ontario highways. A full brake fluid flush restores hydraulic pressure and protects calipers and brake lines from internal corrosion.

  • Recommended every 2 years or 40,000 km
  • Improves pedal feel and response
  • Protects calipers from corrosion
🗜️

Brake Caliper Service

A seized or sticking brake caliper causes uneven pad wear, pulling to one side, and excessive heat buildup. We inspect, lubricate, and replace brake calipers to ensure even, consistent braking across all four corners of your vehicle.

  • Diagnoses pulling and uneven wear
  • Caliper slide pin lubrication and inspection
  • Full caliper replacement when needed

Warning Signs You Need Brake Service

Don't wait until your brakes fail. These are the most common symptoms Waterloo-area drivers describe before coming in for brake work. If you notice any of these, bring your vehicle in for an inspection as soon as possible.

Book an Inspection
  • 🔊
    Squealing or grinding noise when braking Usually worn brake pads. Grinding means metal-on-metal contact — urgent.
  • 📳
    Steering wheel or pedal vibrating when stopping Warped rotors are the most common cause.
  • ↗️
    Car pulling to one side when braking Often a stuck caliper or uneven pad wear.
  • 🦶
    Soft, spongy, or low brake pedal Could indicate air in the brake lines or a fluid leak — stop driving immediately.
  • 🚨
    Brake warning light on the dashboard Don't ignore it. Get an inspection before your next drive.
  • 📅
    More than 2 years since your last brake check Even if everything feels fine, brake components wear silently.
  • 👃
    Burning smell after driving Overheating brakes from a dragging caliper or riding the brake pedal.

Brake Service FAQs

How long does a brake pad replacement take?

Most brake pad replacements at President Tire & Wheels are completed within 1–2 hours. If rotors also need replacing, allow 2–3 hours. Call ahead so we can have your parts in stock.

What brake parts do you use?

We only install ProSeries OE+ premium brake pads and rotors — we don't offer economy or budget alternatives. Every brake job comes with a 3-year warranty.

Do I need new rotors when I replace brake pads?

Not always. During inspection we measure rotor thickness and check for warping or deep scoring. If rotors are within spec, they can be reused with new pads. We'll tell you exactly what we find before recommending anything.

Is it safe to drive with squealing brakes?

Squealing is a wear indicator — your pads are getting thin. You have some time but you shouldn't ignore it. If the noise has turned to grinding, stop driving and call us immediately — metal-on-metal contact damages rotors quickly and becomes a safety hazard.
